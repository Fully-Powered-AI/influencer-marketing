# Fix request to a creator: a draft for the programme lead

Template dated 2026-10-20. The skill fills each bracket from the live check, the programme file and the roster, and gives the draft to the programme lead, who changes what they like and sends it from their own account. The skill never sends it. The message asks for the change and says why; it makes no statement about the product and does not rewrite the creator's own words beyond the disclosure, code or link.

Fill rules:
- Quote what was seen exactly, with where it was (caption position, timecode, frame).
- Quote the requirement from programme section 8 for this platform, or the code and link issued to the creator, exactly.
- The date is the request date plus programme section 8's "Time a creator has to correct a live post after being asked, in days"; the programme lead gives a date only when that line is blank or `[open]`.
- One message per post; list every item that needs changing in it.
- For an audience setting a platform cannot add after posting, do not ask for a toggle; write what the programme lead decided instead.

---

Subject: A change needed on your [platform] [format] for {{brand name}}

Hi [display name from the roster],

Thank you for your [format] for {{brand name}}, live since [posted date]: [post URL].

When we looked at it on [date of the live check], we found:

- [What was seen, quoted, and where. For example: the caption opens with "[first words as posted]" and the "[disclosure words]" label only appears after the "more" cut.]
- [Next item, if any.]

What the programme needs on [platform]:

- [The requirement from programme section 8, quoted, or: your code is [issued code] and your link is [issued link].]

Could you make the change by [due date]? Once it is done, we will look at the post again and let you know it is all set. If anything here does not match what you see, reply and tell us; we would rather check again than guess.

Thanks,
[the programme lead signs with their own name and role when sending]
