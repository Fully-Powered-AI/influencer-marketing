# Removal referral: a draft for the programme lead

Template dated 2026-10-20. For a post found removed at a check inside the campaign window. The skill records the removal, finds the terms that bear on it and hands this note to the programme lead. It does not judge whether the creator broke the agreement and it does not contact the creator. Every bracket is filled from the workspace or written "not on file".

---

Post: [publication_id], [platform] [format], [post URL], live from [posted_at]

Seen removed: at the day [N] check on [date], recorded by [role]. [If the person knows whether it lapsed by itself, as a story can, or was taken down, say so; otherwise "cause not known".]

Campaign window: [start] to [end], from campaigns/[campaign_slug]/plan.md. [Or: "the plan gives no window".]

The agreement: [the file named in the roster's agreement_file], section [n], on [keeping posts live, takedown and correction, or deliverables], quoted: "[the term's words]". [Or: "no agreement file on the roster; the owner holds the signed terms".]

The deal: [cpa, flat, hybrid or gift]. Fee milestone on this post: [reached on date, part, held, or none recorded].

Reuse: rights_until [date or unknown]. [Whether content-reuse has this asset in a plan, if the person knows.]

For the programme lead to decide: whether to ask the creator about it, whether the agreement term applies, and whether anything changes for the fee (the owner's call) or for reuse.

Recorded with: record [publication_id] --removal-reviewed "[one line: inside the window, referred to the programme lead on date, agreement section n]"
