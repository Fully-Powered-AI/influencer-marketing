## 3. Disclosure and the brand's duty to monitor

- A material connection, including commission and free product, must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously (16 CFR 255.5, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5, read 2026-10-20; compliance notes section 1).
- In video the disclosure belongs in the video, spoken and on screen; a platform's own label may not be enough on its own (ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers, read 2026-10-20).
- Advertisers need a reasonable programme to train and monitor endorsers: say which claims are allowed, instruct on disclosure, search what endorsers say, act on problems (ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides, read 2026-10-20; compliance notes section 1).

## 4. Claims

Budget and brief only owner-cleared product statements from programme section 7. Resolve conflicting labels, specifications and public copy before clearing a claim. Do not transfer statements between products or variants.

## 5. The actual store

Obtain the storefront URL, currency, live promotions, shipping thresholds, landing pages, stock and seasonal calendar from the owner. Check pages for placeholders and mismatched offers before sending traffic. No store observations are bundled.

## 7. Verify before launch

Verify current vendor fees and eligibility, the actual checkout discount combinations, order-level attribution exports, current platform rules and all forecast assumptions. The package supplies no conversion-rate benchmark for your category.
