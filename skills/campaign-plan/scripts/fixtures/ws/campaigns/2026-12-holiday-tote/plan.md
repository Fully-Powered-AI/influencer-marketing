## 1. Objective

In this programme's order:

1. Tracked orders and new customers through creators' codes and links, at a cost per tracked order the owner accepts.
2. Content assets with reuse rights the brand can use within their windows.
3. Reach, reported apart and never used to rank creators.

A test plan for the scripts. Targets are the base and conservative cases in section 4.

## 2. KPIs and attribution

Attribution model: a matching code wins; a tracked link without a code is second; nothing else is attributed

Ranking metric: cost per tracked order

| KPI | what it counts | computed from | label when reported | target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tracked orders | orders attributed by the model above, after the holdback | orders export | Measured | section 4 |
| new customers | tracked orders with no earlier order by the same hashed email | orders export | Measured | not projected |
| cost per tracked order | programme cost over tracked orders | statement and orders export | Measured | section 4 |
| commission return on sales | tracked net sales over programme cost | as above | Measured | section 4 |
| posts live with disclosure | live posts with disclosure seen, over posts due | data/publications.csv | Measured | 11 |

| reported apart: reach, views, likes | platform figures | screenshots | Estimated or User-provided | none |
