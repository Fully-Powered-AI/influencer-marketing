## 1. Endorsements the brand reuses

- A connection the audience would not expect, including payment, free or discounted product and affiliate commission, must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously (16 CFR 255.5, https://www.law.cornell.edu/cfr/text/16/255.5; pack notes section 1).
- Clear and conspicuous means hard to miss and easy to understand. It sits with the endorsement, not behind a "more" link or among hashtags. In video it is both heard and shown on screen; on an image it is superimposed long enough to read (16 CFR 255.0(f) and FTC "Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers"; pack notes section 1).
- A platform's built-in disclosure tool is used in addition to the party's own disclosure, not instead of it (FTC "Disclosures 101" and the 2023 Endorsement Guides press release; pack notes section 1).
- Advertisers are liable for misleading or unsubstantiated statements made through endorsements (16 CFR 255.1(d)). An advertiser cannot make through a testimonial a claim it could not make directly (FTC Health Products Compliance Guidance; pack notes section 1).
- The Consumer Reviews and Testimonials Rule (16 CFR Part 465, announced 14 August 2024) bans fake or false reviews and testimonials and undisclosed insider reviews, among other practices (FTC press release; pack notes section 2).
- This skill's reading: content the brand reposts on its own page, email, account, deck or ad is the brand's own advertising, so the claims rule and the connection disclosure bind the brand directly. A paid creator's content placed among customer reviews, under a verified-purchase badge or in product questions and answers would read as an independent customer, which Part 465 is aimed at.

## 2. Product claims inside an asset

Preserve the product, scope and qualifiers of every approved statement. A cut, overlay or product tag can change what a claim means; send such changes to content-approval. Programme section 7 is the source of cleared wording. Resolve conflicts in owner-supplied product evidence before reuse.

## 3. Paid use by the brand, per platform

Paid reuse needs an agreement grant and current permission for that platform, category, market and placement. Programme section 3 records the verified decision. Separate creator sponsorship from brand advertising and commerce. Historical restricted-category examples are in shared policy notes section 5; they do not rule other categories.

## 4. Audience settings

Apply programme section 9, including an explicit none where appropriate. Verify platform controls at use when a restriction is required. An unavailable control is an unresolved dependency; do not assume it waives the rule.

## 5. Shopify and commerce surfaces

- Shopify Collabs may open a dispute automatically for violations such as "discount code leaks or paid ads activity"; an unresolved dispute pauses the connection after 30 days and cancels the commission, links and codes (https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/promoting-marketing/collabs/merchants/payments; pack rails section 1). This skill's reading: a brand ad that shows a creator's Collabs code could be read as paid ads activity; confirm with Shopify before running one.
- Verify commerce-surface eligibility for the actual category; permission to advertise does not establish permission to list products (dated source leads: pack rails section 2).
- The orders export carries the code used ("Discount Code"); the code is the attribution that survives blocked cookies (pack rails section 6). This skill's reading: a creator's code left visible in a reused asset will attribute brand-channel sales to that creator.

## 6. Not in the references; verify

- Creator-identity ad formats (Meta partnership ads, TikTok Spark Ads, whitelisting) for the configured category.
- Whether a platform lets a brand put its own paid-partnership label on its own repost of a creator's content.
- Whether the brand's own organic posts about its own products fall under a platform's branded-content rules.
- Whether Instagram Shopping, Facebook Shop, TikTok Shop or YouTube Shopping accept this category, and whether the brand's catalogue is approved on any of them.
- Ad specifications (sizes, lengths, file limits) for any platform.
