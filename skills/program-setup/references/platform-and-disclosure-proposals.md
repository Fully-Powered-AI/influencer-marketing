# Platform, disclosure and audience decisions

## 1. Section 3: per-platform decisions

For the actual product category and market, verify the current policy for organic sponsorship, paid amplification and commerce separately. Use the source URLs in `../../../references/platform-and-compliance-notes.md` as dated leads. No row is pre-approved or prohibited by this template. Record `unknown` when evidence is missing, with a role to resolve it. An owner's preference does not verify a platform rule.

Record `with conditions: <specific conditions>` when applicable; write label and audience evidence in the fourth column and the source/date in the last. Use supported roster platforms from `../../../references/schemas.md`. Treat `other` as the configured catch-all and state which platform it means.

## 2. Section 8: disclosure

Verify the required wording and placement for the actual compensation, market, platform and medium. The owner clears one exact caption wording per platform; the spoken form is separate. Example for the fictional Cedar Lane brand:

```markdown
  - youtube: "#ad" at the start of the description; spoken: this video is sponsored by Cedar Lane; shown on screen at the beginning; platform disclosure tool on [owner]
```

This illustrates parser shape, not a universal legal prescription. Record non-counting words, live-check role and correction window. A code or a platform tool does not automatically substitute for the required wording. Dated source observations remain in the shared policy notes; verify their current applicability.

## 3. Section 9: audience and content suitability

The owner records the category's actual audience requirement and evidence. An ordinary product may have `none; <reason> [owner]`. A category with a verified age restriction records the minimum and evidence needed on the relevant platform. A required setting that is unavailable is unresolved; do not silently waive it. Separate creator contracting eligibility from audience targeting. Neither the package nor a public profile verifies a person's age.
